# Scalar website refinement

Plan revision 1, 2026-09-13, recorded before implementation.

Repository: mosnin/Sicarii. Mode: product_refinement. Owner/reviewer: Codex, self-review.

## Intended outcome
Research prospects and prepare the next follow-up from one CRM.
Founders and sales operators; discover, verify identity, enrich, review and follow up.

## Visual plan
Preserve Baby blue and white, Space Grotesk, existing ASCII field, panel composition and motion.
Hierarchy: first a concrete customer job, second the product mechanism and conditions, third one action to begin or inspect a relevant workflow. Pricing exposes the total commitment before an action.
Composition: extend existing page primitives and grids. Product pages explain capabilities; solution pages explain a buyer's workflow; guides support successful first use. Reuse the established navigation shell and footer.
Material: preserve supplied backgrounds, graphics, fonts, borders and motion. No new visual identity or decorative stock assets.
Details: complete sentences, explicit units and billing intervals, no invented savings, customers, testimonials or guaranteed business results. Label sample records and proposed plans. Focus indicators and disclosure states stay visible.
Responsive: inspect 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 widths; pricing stacks without clipped units; menus retain keyboard and touch access, Escape and focus recovery; reduced motion keeps content visible.
Alternative: a replacement landing template would reduce work but discard the user's chosen visual identity. Rejected. Reuse current page compositions and make their explanations specific.

## Evidence and limits

Customer interviews, logo permissions, provider invoices and subscription activation evidence are unknown. Pricing is a reviewable proposal where it changes existing terms. Do not change billing, entitlement enforcement or existing customers.
