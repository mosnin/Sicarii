# Write cold email that replies

## The pattern (every mail)
- Under 90 words. One reader, one problem, one ask.
- Subject: 1-5 words, lowercase, no pitch (the preview line is the real subject).
- First line: the prospect (their company, role, signal) — never yourself.
- One concrete proof (metric, customer, observation), not adjectives.
- One CTA, answerable with "yes" (a time, a question, a teardown offer).
- PS line: the second most clickable line — put the hook there, not the fluff.

## Personalization that counts
- {{firstName}} {{company}} tokens are table stakes. One sentence must prove
  you looked: a job post, a launch, a tool they use, a hiring signal.
- If you can't write that sentence from the CRM record, enrich first or skip
  the contact. A generic blast burns the domain for everyone.

## Grade before you queue (all must pass)
1. Could this mail only have been sent to THIS person? (no → rewrite)
2. Is the ask answerable in under 10 seconds? (no → simplify)
3. Does it read fine on a phone preview (subject + first 12 words)? (no → cut)
4. One CTA only? (two → delete one)
5. No links in mail one? (links tank deliverability on cold mail one)
6. Unsubscribe + physical-address footer present? (missing → add)

## Replies
- A reply pauses the sequence automatically. Answer the human like a human:
  short, specific, no deck unless asked. Move the pipeline entry and close the
  loop in the CRM the same day.
